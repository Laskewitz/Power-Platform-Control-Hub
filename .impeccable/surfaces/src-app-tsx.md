## Scope and mode

Whole authenticated application shell and all nested surfaces. Operate mode.

## Audience and job

Power Platform tenant administrators triaging risk, investigating tenant resources, and completing governance actions.

## Task and content

Move from tenant scope or an admin signal to the relevant entity, understand its context, and take an appropriate action. Preserve all inventory, environment, connector, licensing, capacity, DLP, policy, recommendation, best-practice, and entity-management workflows.

## Constraints

Preserve Microsoft terminology, Power Apps host behavior, accessibility, responsive layouts, light/dark themes, Power Platform Advocates attribution, and truthful upstream API limitations.

The memorable moment is the overview's wall of active tenant monitors, where real inventory and recommendation data reads as live telemetry.
